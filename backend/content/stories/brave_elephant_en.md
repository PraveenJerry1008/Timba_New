In a green forest near the Kaveri river lived a baby elephant named Chinnu.
Chinnu loved playing with his friends, but every time thunder rumbled in the
sky, he ran to hide behind his mother's legs.

One rainy evening, a little rabbit got stuck under a fallen branch during a
storm, crying for help. All the older animals were too far away. Chinnu was
scared, his ears trembling with every thunderclap, but he thought of the
rabbit alone and frightened.

He took a deep breath, walked through the rain, and gently lifted the branch
with his trunk. The rabbit hopped free and hugged him. That night, Chinnu
realized something: being brave doesn't mean you're not scared — it means
you help anyway, even when your knees are shaking.

From then on, whenever thunder rolled across the sky, Chinnu remembered the
rabbit's smile, and it didn't feel quite so scary anymore.
