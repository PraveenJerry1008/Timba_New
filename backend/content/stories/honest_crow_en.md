A young crow named Kaaka was playing near a farmer's clay water pot and
accidentally knocked it over, cracking it. No one saw. Kaaka could have
flown away and said nothing.

Instead, Kaaka waited for the farmer and said, "I broke your pot while
playing. I'm sorry — I'll help you carry water until you get a new one."

The farmer was surprised, then smiled. "Most creatures would have hidden
this. Because you told the truth, I'm not even angry anymore." He gave
Kaaka a handful of grain for being honest.

Kaaka learned that the truth feels heavy to say, but it makes the heart
light afterward — much lighter than carrying a secret ever could.
